OPEN IT
Extract the ZIP first, then double-click index.html.
No installation, account, build step, or local server is needed.
Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js, and the assets folder together.

The design, images, font, and project breakdowns work offline.
Demo videos, GitHub, Steam, LinkedIn, and the résumé open on their original
websites and need an internet connection. Email links open your mail app.

PUT IT ONLINE
Upload index.html, styles.css, script.js, and assets/ to any static web host.

This is a standalone HTML site; it is not a Framer project import.
Nothing has been published and the existing aidanstraker.com is unchanged.
